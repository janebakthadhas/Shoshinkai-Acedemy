About the Art

Shorin Ryu
Matsubayashi Karate

The art of Karate was developed across the islands of Okinawa. The style which emerged in Shuri came to be known as Shorin Ryu — emphasising speed, breathing, coordination, and internal peace. The Matsubayashi branch was founded by O'Sensei Shoshin Nagamine, one of the most revered karate masters of the 20th century.

Grandmaster Katsuo Shiroma trained directly under O'Sensei Nagamine and, after his passing, founded the Bukenkan — meaning "Art Focus School." The Bukenkan of the USA carries this authentic Okinawan lineage onto American soil with the same unwavering devotion to tradition.

Our mission is to pass the art forward in its purest form — developing not only skilled martial artists, but strong character, discipline, and lifelong community.

Technique
技

A punch is never just a punch. It is a thousand repetitions distilled into one clean moment. Technique is the pursuit of precision — not the brute force of muscle, but the quiet authority of a movement that wastes nothing.

In Shorin-Ryu, we practice kata not to perform, but to ingrain. Each stance, each strike, each transition becomes vocabulary. And when the vocabulary is fluent, the body speaks without thought.

Perfect technique is not about power. It is about inevitability.

Founding Lineage

The Grandmasters

10th Dan — Red Belt
🥋
Hanshi. Shoshin Nagamine
Founder — Shorinryu Matsubayashi Karate

Hanshi Shoshin Nagamine (1907–1997) founded Shorin Ryu Matsubayashi Karate Do. He studied under Ankichi Arakaki and Chotoku Kyan Sensei. Practicing Karate for 75 of his 90 years, he earned world recognition and served as the first president of the Okinawa Shorin Ryu Karate Association.

10th Dan Red Belt — Okinawa, Japan Founder, Shorinryu Matsubayashi Karate First President, Okinawa Shorin Ryu Karate Association
10th Dan — Red Belt
🥋
Hanshi. Katsuo Shiroma
GrandMaster — Bukenkan Karate

Born in Okinawa, Hanshi Shiroma began training at age 14 under Shimu Masau and became a dedicated pupil of O'Sensei Nagamine. Following his master's passing he founded the Bukenkan — "Art Focus School" — continuing the lineage with absolute fidelity.

10th Dan Red Belt — Okinawa, Japan GrandMaster — Japan World President, Bukenkan Karate
